# WORLD MAP

## Vertical slice map
The first playable map should be small enough to understand in minutes.

### Suggested zones
1. **Residential block** — introduction and environmental storytelling.
2. **Industrial corridor** — abandoned infrastructure and first signs of automation.
3. **Resistance safehouse** — human hub and mission briefing.
4. **Abandoned facility** — primary terminal/infiltration location.
5. **Extraction route** — escape after SYNAPSE detects the intrusion.

## Expansion zones
Later maps may include border infrastructure, industrial complexes, hills, transport corridors, damaged commercial districts, strategic facilities and rural/peripheral areas.

## Map philosophy
The city should feel connected and geographically plausible without requiring a full simulation of Tijuana. Use recognizable environmental cues rather than exact real-world replication.
