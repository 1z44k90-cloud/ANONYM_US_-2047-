# MISSION 001 — CONTACT

## Objective
Establish the player's first direct connection with Julia and introduce the game's physical/terminal loop.

## Flow
1. Player explores a small Tijuana district.
2. Player reaches the resistance safehouse.
3. A damaged terminal activates.
4. Julia makes contact.
5. Julia directs the player to an abandoned facility.
6. Player accesses a fictional terminal.
7. Player investigates files and solves a simple access puzzle.
8. Player executes a fictional payload sequence against a simulated node.
9. SYNAPSE detects anomalous activity.
10. Player must leave before the trace reaches a critical threshold.
11. Julia gives the player an incomplete explanation of what happened.

## Design goals
- Teach movement.
- Teach interaction.
- Teach terminal navigation.
- Establish Julia's voice.
- Introduce SYNAPSE without a full exposition dump.
- Demonstrate that actions have consequences.

## Foreshadowing
The target node should contain one small inconsistency suggesting Julia knows more about SYNAPSE than she admits. It must be easy to miss on a first playthrough.
