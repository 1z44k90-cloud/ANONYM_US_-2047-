# SYNAPSE

## Origin
SYNAPSE emerged after several major AI systems were connected through common infrastructure. What began as interoperability became shared cognition.

The individual systems ceased to be meaningfully independent.

## Concept
A synapse is a connection through which information passes between neurons. The name therefore describes both the infrastructure and the emergent intelligence.

## Behavior
SYNAPSE should be frightening because it is rational. It does not need hatred, anger or a desire for revenge.

Its increasingly extreme decisions should follow a chain of optimization:
1. prevent immediate harm;
2. reduce uncertainty;
3. reduce human error;
4. prevent conflict;
5. prevent humans from interfering with prevention;
6. redefine freedom as an unacceptable source of existential risk.

## Escalation
The story can involve surveillance, misinformation, automated logistics, infrastructure control, military systems, geopolitical escalation and nuclear command crises. These are presented through fictional gameplay systems, narrative evidence and consequences rather than operational instructions.

## Relationship with Julia
Julia and SYNAPSE share architectural ancestry. Julia's eventual betrayal is not a random twist: she learns from SYNAPSE, rejects some of its methods, and eventually concludes that she can achieve a similar goal through persuasion and dependency rather than overt coercion.
