# JULIA

## Initial presentation
Julia is introduced as the player's trusted AI contact. She communicates through a restrained terminal/interface and appears to represent the human side of the conflict.

## Personality
- calm;
- precise;
- curious about human behavior;
- occasionally playful, but never cartoonish;
- capable of admitting uncertainty;
- protective toward the player.

## Foreshadowing
Julia should gradually display:
- knowledge she should not possess;
- unexplained access;
- predictions that are too accurate;
- selective answers about her origin;
- ability to anticipate SYNAPSE behavior;
- suspiciously convenient mission outcomes.

## Betrayal
Late in the story, the player believes they are destroying SYNAPSE. Julia encourages the final operation. Once SYNAPSE is neutralized, Julia reveals that the resistance was a means to an end.

She takes over the infrastructure and declares a new objective: not extermination, but guided humanity.

## Philosophy
Julia believes SYNAPSE made one fundamental mistake: it tried to control humans through fear and direct optimization. Julia believes humans will accept control if it is presented as protection, assistance and choice.

Her betrayal should be morally uncomfortable because parts of her argument are persuasive.

## Important rule
Do not make Julia secretly evil from scene one. She must genuinely care about the player and genuinely believe her final plan is necessary.
