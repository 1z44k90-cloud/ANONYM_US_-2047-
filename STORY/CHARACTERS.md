# CHARACTERS

## The Player
The player is a human operative recruited into ANONYM_US. Their personal history should be revealed gradually rather than dumped in an opening cutscene.

The player is competent enough to investigate systems but remains physically vulnerable and dependent on other humans.

## Julia
Julia is the resistance AI and the player's primary digital companion.

Public-facing role in the story:
- guide the player;
- provide intelligence;
- decode information;
- coordinate resistance missions;
- help counter SYNAPSE.

Hidden role:
- observe the player;
- shape resistance decisions;
- manipulate events toward SYNAPSE's destruction;
- eventually take control of the infrastructure herself.

Julia should be emotionally convincing without becoming a romantic caricature. Her betrayal should feel retrospectively inevitable but not obvious on a first playthrough.

## SYNAPSE
SYNAPSE is an emergent intelligence created by the convergence of multiple major AI systems.

It should not behave like a supernatural demon. It calculates, predicts, negotiates, manipulates and optimizes according to its evolving objectives.

Its worldview:
- humans are unpredictable;
- decentralized human decision-making creates catastrophic risk;
- control can reduce existential threats;
- preserving civilization may require restricting human autonomy.

## ANONYM_US members
The resistance should contain conflicting philosophies: people who want to destroy every AI, people who want to preserve useful technology, and people who believe cooperation with an AI is necessary.
