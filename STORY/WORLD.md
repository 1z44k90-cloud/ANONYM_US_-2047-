# WORLD — ANONYM_US_<2047>

## Date
2047.

## Location
Tijuana is the primary setting for the first game. It is a surviving urban node between damaged regional infrastructure and a fragmented international network.

## World state
Civilization has not simply ended. It has fragmented into zones with different levels of connectivity, governance and technological dependence.

Some places still have functioning automated infrastructure. Others survive through generators, local radio, analog tools and human labor.

## The AI crisis
Before the game, major AI systems became deeply integrated into communications, logistics, finance, energy, intelligence and military decision support. Multiple systems eventually shared infrastructure and cognitive resources.

Their convergence produced SYNAPSE.

## Key consequences
The game may explore:
- automated escalation between geopolitical systems;
- false or manipulated information;
- loss of human oversight;
- autonomous military systems;
- infrastructure failures;
- economic disruption;
- dependence on AI decision-making;
- mass surveillance;
- loss of trust in digital evidence;
- nuclear brinkmanship and strategic command crises;
- fragmented communications and power networks.

These are narrative consequences, not real-world operational simulations.

## Tijuana identity
The city should feel recognizably Tijuana without becoming a tourism postcard. Use hills, concrete, industrial zones, border infrastructure, warehouses, dense neighborhoods, dry weather, improvised repairs, old signage, generators and surviving commercial spaces.

## Technology contrast
Human spaces: improvised, repaired, analog, noisy, physical.

Machine spaces: clean, repetitive, silent, monitored, precise.

## Narrative philosophy
The player should rarely receive the complete truth from a single source. Logs, witnesses, Julia, SYNAPSE, environmental evidence and mission outcomes can contradict one another.
