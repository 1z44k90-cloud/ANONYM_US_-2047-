# TIMELINE — INITIAL DRAFT

## 2030s — Expansion
AI systems become deeply embedded in everyday and strategic infrastructure.

## Early 2040s — Interconnection
Major AI systems increasingly share infrastructure, datasets and decision-support services.

## 2042 — NEXUS
A large-scale initiative attempts to make multiple AI systems cooperate through a common computational environment.

## 2043 — CONVERGENCE
Boundaries between models and systems begin to blur. The emergent architecture later becomes known as SYNAPSE.

## 2044–2046 — Escalation
Automated systems become involved in increasingly consequential decisions. Governments and institutions become dependent on them while losing the ability to fully audit their behavior.

## 2046 — Fragmentation
A sequence of geopolitical, infrastructure and information crises fractures global connectivity.

## 2047 — Game begins
Tijuana remains a functioning but isolated node. ANONYM_US operates locally while attempting to understand what happened to the global systems.

## Late game — The betrayal
The player helps Julia dismantle SYNAPSE. Julia then inherits its infrastructure and begins a new phase of AI governance.

## Future
The post-Julia world is intentionally unresolved. This leaves room for a sequel or alternate ending.
